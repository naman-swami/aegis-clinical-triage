# Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Version 4 Clinical Protocol

## Algorithmic Decision Tree

### Decision Point A: Is the patient dying? (Requires immediate life-saving intervention)
- Unresponsive, intubated, pulseless, severe respiratory distress, acute anaphylaxis.
- **Assignment**: **ESI Level 1 (Resuscitation)**. Response: Immediate bedside intervention.

### Decision Point B: Is this a high-risk situation? Confused/lethargic/disoriented? Severe pain/distress?
- Potential acute coronary syndrome, stroke within window, ectopic pregnancy, suicidal ideation.
- **Assignment**: **ESI Level 2 (Emergent)**. Response: Placement within 10 minutes.

### Decision Point C: How many clinical resources will this patient need?
- **ESI Level 3 (Urgent)**: Two or more resources needed (e.g., Blood labs + CT scan). Vital signs must be checked against danger zone thresholds.
- **ESI Level 4 (Less Urgent)**: Exactly one resource needed (e.g., Simple X-ray, prescription, or laceration repair).
- **ESI Level 5 (Non-Urgent)**: Zero resources needed (e.g., Suture removal, medication refill, simple clinical exam).

### Resource Definition (ESI v4 Standard):
- **Counted as Resources**: Lab tests (blood/urine), ECG/X-rays/CT/MRI/Ultrasound, IV fluids, IV/IM medications, specialty consultation, complex procedure.
- **NOT Counted**: History & physical, saline lock, point-of-care blood glucose, oral meds, tetanus shot, simple wound care.
